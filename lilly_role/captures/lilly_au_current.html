For over 150 years, we have worked tirelessly to develop and deliver trusted medicines that meet real needs.
Australian laws and regulations limit the amount of product information that we can provide to the general public via our website. We encourage patients wishing to obtain more detailed information about a Lilly product that has been prescribed to them, to speak to their healthcare professional. The product information on this website is intended for Australian residents, if you are looking for our New Zealand products page, please click here.
