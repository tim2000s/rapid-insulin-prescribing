Working on a news story, newsletter, digital content, podcast or other media project involving Lilly or our products?
Submit your request below and a member of our communications team will respond as soon as possible. Send requests for b-roll or executive biographies to b-roll@lilly.com .
Please do not submit requests for donations, sponsorships, employment or medical advice via this form.
Non-press or media-related inquiries can be directed to Lilly’s Contact Us page or call Lilly Support Services (1-800-LillyRX) for assistance.
