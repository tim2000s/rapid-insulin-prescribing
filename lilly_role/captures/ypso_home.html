myLoop

The future of  diabetes management

The automated insulin delivery system that runs from your smartphone

Smartphone-based therapy management is the future of diabetes treatment. Combine the YpsoPump insulin pump, the CamAPS FX mobile application and the Dexcom G7, FreeStyle Libre 3 or FreeStyle Libre 3 Plus Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) sensor for an automated insulin delivery system managed directly from your smartphone. The integration of automated insulin delivery into the YpsoPump system portfolio allows you time away from your diabetes and more freedom to do what you love in life.

  • Adaptive and self-learning
