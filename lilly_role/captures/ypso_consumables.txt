Infusion sets 
We will send a 3 month supply (approximate) of your chosen infusion sets. If you need to order a larger quantity, please call us on 0344 856 7820. 
 Do not place an order for infusion sets if you are on standing order. If you are not sure, give the office a call on 0344 856 7820.
