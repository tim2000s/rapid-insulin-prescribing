 Advice
following an abbreviated submission: 
 insulin aspart (Fiasp®) is accepted for use within NHS Scotland. 
 Indication under review: treatment of diabetes mellitus in adults. 
Insulin aspart (Fiasp®) is a new formulation with a faster onset of action than another formulation of insulin aspart and is available at an equivalent cost.
