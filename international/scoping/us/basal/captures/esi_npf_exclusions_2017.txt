2017 Preferred Drug List Exclusions
The excluded medications shown below are not covered on the Express Scripts drug list. In most cases, if you fill
a prescription for one of these drugs, you will pay the full retail price.

Take action to avoid paying full price. If you’re currently using one of the excluded medications, please ask your
doctor to consider writing you a new prescription for one of the following preferred alternatives.
