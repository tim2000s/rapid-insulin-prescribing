HRT Initiative
Free Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) commenced
on the 1st June 2025 for all women experiencing
menopause. The HSE covers the cost of HRT medicines
and products prescribed by a GP and the pharmacy
dispensing fee for each item. To access, women need
to sign up for a Drugs Payment Scheme card if they
do not hold a medical card. For medical cardholders,
no prescription charge applies to HRT medicines and
products on the reimbursement list.
