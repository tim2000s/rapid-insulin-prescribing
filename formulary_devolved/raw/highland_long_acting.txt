INSULIN DETEMIR 
 Important: 
 Formulation and dosage details
 Formulation:
 Injection 100 units/mL (specialist use only)
 3mL cartridge (Levemir ®  for NovoPen ®  6  or NovoPen ® Echo Plus)
 3mL pre-filled disposable injection device (Levemir ®  FlexPen ® ) 
 Dosage:
 As per SMC 1126/16 : for treatment of diabetes mellitus in adults, adolescents and children aged 1 year and above. 
 SMC restriction: in patients unable to achieve good glycaemic control with established insulins.
INSULIN GLARGINE 
 Important: 
 Therapy notes
 Insulin glargine 300 units/mL (Toujeo ® ) has similar efficacy but is not bioequivalent to insulin glargine 100 units/mL (Abasaglar ® , Lantus ® ) and is therefore not interchangeable without dose adjustment. When changing to Toujeo ® , patients should start with the same insulin glargine dose then a small dose titration may be needed.
