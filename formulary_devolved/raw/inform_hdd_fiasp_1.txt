 Insulin aspart 
 06.01.01.01 (Short-acting insulins) > [Recombinant human insulin analogue] 
 Date added to formulary : 02-08-2017 
 Formulary Status:
 S    -  Specialist initiated   
 Medicines Available :
 Insulin aspart (Fiasp) Fiasp ® Insulin 
   • Fiasp 100units/ml solution for injection 10ml vials 
   • Fiasp FlexTouch 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen 
   • Fiasp Penfill 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges 
 Following submission of an evaluation of use by the Specialist Diabetic Team FIASP has been reclassifed. 
 JP March 2019 
 Fiasp ® is approved for a limited number ( type I diabetes with persistent post prandial hyperglycaemia ). Patients will be carefully assessed and monitored during the trial period and aware that they will revert to NovoRapid unless a demonstrable benefit is shown. Initiation, monitoring and supply will be from secondary care only during the trial period. Care must be taken to ensure that all patients receive the correct brand of insulin aspart. 
 JP July 2017
