 Link to the West of Scotland Regional Endocrine System Formulary Chapter 
 The NHS Lanarkshire Joint Adult Formulary is currently undergoing review for transition to the West of Scotland Regional Formulary. As the West of Scotland Regional Formulary chapters are launched, links will be added to the Lanarkshire Formulary to re-direct users to the new site. Please see the West of Scotland Regional Formulary website for more information. 
 Please direct any Formulary queries to the Medicines Policy and Guidance team via the email address medsguidance@lanarkshire.scot.nhs.uk.
