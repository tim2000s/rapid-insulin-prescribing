Insulin aspart
06.01.01.01 (Short-acting insulins)
Date added to formulary : 27-11-2023
Formulary Status:
G     -  Green
Medicines Available :
Fast acting Insulin aspart (Fiasp)
It is advised that Fiasp® should be prescribed as brand (as the rINN3 for both Fiasp® and NovoRapid® is insulin aspart) to avoid potential confusion:
Note difference between Fiasp and NovoRapid
Fiasp  - fast-acting insulin aspart
NovoRapid  - conventional insulin aspart prescribe as the brands only
Fiasp® was suitable for initiation by non-specialist prescribers (designated Green in the local Traffic Light system), this was consistent with the current advice for NovoRapid®.
Biosimilar insulin aspart "Trurapi" is also approved for use within ABUHB
(MTC July 2017)
(Fast Acting Insulin Aspart) (Fiasp)
• Fiasp 100units/ml solution for injection 10ml vials
• Fiasp FlexTouch 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
• Fiasp Penfill 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
