Insulin lispro
06.01.01.01 (Short-acting insulins) > [Recombinant human insulin analogue] Date added to formulary : 24-03-2021
Formulary Status: S 1st - Specialist initiated 1st Line

Medicines Available:
Lyumjev 100 (insulin lispro)
Lyumjev is a mealtime insulin for subcutaneous administration just before (0-2 minutes) the start of the meal, with the option to administer within 20 minutes of starting the meal in T1DM or T2DM patients.
 • Lyumjev 100units/ml solution for injection 10ml vials
 • Lyumjev 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
 • Lyumjev KwikPen 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pens
 • Lyumjev Junior KwikPen 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pens
Prescribing Notes
Prescribe all insulin and insulin combination products by BRAND, specifying STRENGTH of the product and the DEVICE
