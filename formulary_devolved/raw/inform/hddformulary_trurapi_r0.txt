Insulin aspart
06.01.01.01 (Short-acting insulins) > [Recombinant human insulin analogue]
Date added to formulary : 10-07-2024
Formulary Status:
S    -  Specialist initiated
Medicines Available :
Insulin aspart Trurapi/Novorapid
Specialist Initiated (please note that this includes GPs who initiate insulin)
NICE guideline NG17  and NICE guideline NG28  state - When starting an insulin for which a biosimilar is available, use the product with the lowest acquisition cost.
Switching Novorapid Flexpen to Trurapi Solostar
Trurapi is a biosimilar of the rapid-acting insulin NovoRapid . It is manufactured and marketed by Sanofi.
Cost saving initiative started in September 2024. Project will
Switch patients from pre-filled pens (Novorapid Flexpens to Trurapi Solostar)
NOT switch those using a Novopen reusable cartridge pen as this has smart connectivity, while the Sanofi Allstar pens are not connected, and we wouldn’t want to remove that functionality from people who are using it.
It would of course be fine for diabetes professionals to change individual patients if you know they are not using the smart features of their current pen.
Patients will be sent information leaflets and letters explaining the change.  The leaflets can be accessed here:
Trurapi_SoloStar_Guide.pdf (mysanofiinsulin.co.uk)  and in Welsh  Trurapi User Guide WELSH (mysanofiinsulin.co.uk)
The protocol we will be following is here
[SEB 27th December 2024]
• Trurapi 100units/ml solution for injection 10ml vials
• Trurapi 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
• Trurapi 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled Solostar pens
