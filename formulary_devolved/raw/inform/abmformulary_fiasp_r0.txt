Insulin aspart (Fiasp)
Please prescribe insulin by brand.
Specialist initiation only.
Approved for use in SBUHB in children and young people with type 1 DM in accordance with NICE NG18
• Fiasp 100units/ml solution for injection 10ml vials
• Fiasp FlexTouch 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
• Fiasp Penfill 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
Please note insulin analogues are second line formulary choices after human insulin products- prescribe in accordance with NICE guidance on use of insulin.
