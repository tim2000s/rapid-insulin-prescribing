Insulin lispro
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins) > [ Biphasic insulins ]
Date added to formulary : 03-05-2017
Formulary Status:
A     -  Amber
Medicines Available :
Insulin lispro ( Humalog KwikPen 200units/ml)
User of insulin lispro 200units/ml may be considered as an option only where patients are experiencing significant problems due to the volume of the insulin being injected (due to the high dose of the 100units/mL insulin). It should only be initiated by specialists in diabetes (this includes accredited GPs with a specialist interest in diabetes and DSN non medical prescribers.
MHRA advice on high strength insulins  (April 2015).
