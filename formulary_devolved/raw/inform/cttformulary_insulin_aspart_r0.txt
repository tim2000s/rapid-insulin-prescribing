Fiasp (Insulin aspart)
Fiasp ® is a mealtime insulin for subcutaneous administration just before (0-2 minutes) the start of the meal, with the option to administer within 20 minutes of starting the meal in T1DM or T2DM patients.
It is an option to improve control of post-prandial hyperglycaemia, particularly where the timing of the injection relative to meal is restricted whether it be as a result of occupation, or any condition which may interfere with normal gastrointestinal absorption.
This includes pregnancy and insulin pump use where necessary.
