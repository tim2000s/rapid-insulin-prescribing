# EU and EEA withdrawals of Fiasp, Lyumjev and comparator insulins

`eu_withdrawals.csv` lists announced, proposed and completed withdrawals and shortages since 2017,
read on 1 October 2026, with the reason each source gives quoted exactly and its URL.
`build_eu_withdrawals.py` regenerates it from the page captures; `html2txt.py` converts captures to
text. The captures (about 25 MB, including news articles and one register whose data carry a
copyright notice) are kept locally and not published here; every row cites its public source URL.
`manual/` holds the EMA medicine shortage communication for Lilly's 2026 discontinuations (adopted
16 March 2026) and the German AMK notice 18/26, fetched separately.
