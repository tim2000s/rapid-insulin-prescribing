Insulin degludec
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins) > [Insulin degludec] Date added to formulary : no date recorded
Formulary Status: S 1st - Specialist initiated 1st Line

Medicines Available:
Tresiba (insulin degludec)
AWMSG Advice No 1822
Insulin degludec (Tresiba®) is recommended as an option for use within NHS Wales for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adults, adolescents and children from the age of 1 year.

This advice incorporates and replaces the AWMSG recommendation for insulin degludec (Tresiba®) for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adult patients where treatment with a basal insulin analogue is considered appropriate (advice number 2816, originally published October 2016).

AWMSG Advice No 1822 - 4.10.22
 
 •Tresiba Penfill 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
 •Tresiba FlexTouch 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
 •Insulin degludec 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges (Tresiba 100)
 •Insulin degludec 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled disposable devices (Tresiba 100)
Prescribing Notes
Prescribe all insulin and insulin combination products by BRAND, specifying STRENGTH of the product and the DEVICE
