Medicines Available :
Insulin degludec
(Tresiba ® )
Specialist Initiated (including insulin-initiating GPs)   to be prescribed in accordance with AWMSG recommendations:
AWMSG (Ref 5196) -  Insulin degludec (Tresiba ® ) is recommended as an option for use within NHS Wales for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adults, adolescents and children from the age of 1 year. (Published October 2022, MMSB October 2022).
• Tresiba FlexTouch 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
• Tresiba Penfill 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
• Tresiba FlexTouch 200units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
Specialist initiation only. Please note insulin analogues are second line formulary choices after human insulin products- prescribe in accordance with NICE guidance on use of insulin
