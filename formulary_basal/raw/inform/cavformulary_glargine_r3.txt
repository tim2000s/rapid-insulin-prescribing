Insulin glargine
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins) > [HIGHER STRENGTH INTERMEDIATE & LONG ]
Date added to formulary : 22-06-2020
Formulary Status:
S    -  Specialist initiated
Medicines Available :
insulin glargine (Toujeo) 300units/ml
**Caution - higher strength product**
Specialist Initiated for treatment of diabetes mellitus.
• Insulin glargine 300units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled disposable devices
• Toujeo 300units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled DoubleStar pen
Prescribing Notes
Prescribe insulins and devices by brand name  in line with  NICE guidance  & ensure patients receive an administration device they have been trained to use. Manufacturers advise any switch between brands or formulation of insulin should be done under strict supervision as change in dose may be required.
Insulins are biologic medicines – biologic and biosimilar medicines should always be prescribed by brand name.  SPS - Medicines to prescribe by brand name in primary care- 2024
