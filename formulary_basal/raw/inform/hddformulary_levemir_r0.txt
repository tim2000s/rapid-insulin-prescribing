Actions in response to the withdrawal of Levemir Insulin 1 August 2025
The attached memo outlines the actions for GP practices to take to identify patients and alternative insulins based on stock availability. Stock availability will be monitored and the memo will be updated on a regular basis.
PCDO guidance.                                                                                                                           [SEB 28th August 2025]
NovoNordisk have announced the discontinuation of Levemir preparations  (MSN/2025/036) . They anticipate that supplies will be exhausted by December 2026.  Do not start new patients on Levemir. Advice on switching patients will be published in due course. Refer patients to their Diabetic Team for further advice.                                                              [SEB 18th June 2025]
