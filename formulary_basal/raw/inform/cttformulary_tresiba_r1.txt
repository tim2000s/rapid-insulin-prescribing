Insulin degludec
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins) > [Insulin degludec]
Date added to formulary : 27-11-2017
Formulary Status:
S   1 st  -  Specialist initiated   1st Line
Medicines Available :
Tresiba (insulin degludec) 200 units/ml
Caution: high strength product
Recommended as an option for restricted use within NHS Wales for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adult patients where treatment with a basal insulin analogue is considered appropriate. Insulin degludec (Tresiba®) is not recommended for use within NHS Wales for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adolescents and children from the age of 1 year.
See AWMSG advice
