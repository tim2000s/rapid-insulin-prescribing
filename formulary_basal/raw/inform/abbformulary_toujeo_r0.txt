Insulin glargine
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins)
Date added to formulary : no date recorded
Formulary Status:
A     -  Amber
Medicines Available :
Insulin glargine 300units/mL ( Toujeo )
1. Use of  Toujeo may be considered only where patients are experiencing significant problems due to the volume of the insulin being injected (due to the high dose of the 100units/mL insulin). This may include patients having to use divided doses of long-acting insulin analogues but who require administration by a third party such as a District Nurse.
2.  Toujeo should only be initiated by specialists in diabetes (this includes accredited GPs with a specialist interest in diabetes and DSN non medical prescribers).
3. Prescribers should always include the strength in units/mL on the prescription when prescribing any pen device of insulin glargine – CHMP’s educational guide advises the brand Toujeo should be written.
4. Patients transferred should be instructed to closely monitor their glucose levels during the transfer and in the following weeks. Toujeo is not bioequivalent to insulin glargine 100units/ml ( Lantus ) and dose adjustment is needed.
5. Letters informing GP practices that a patient has been transferred onto a high dose insulin should indicate this clearly (through use of bold type) early on in the text.
MHRA advice on high strength insulins  (April 2015).
• Toujeo 300units/mL solution for injection 1.5mL pre-filled disposable devices
• Toujeo 300units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled DoubleStar pen
