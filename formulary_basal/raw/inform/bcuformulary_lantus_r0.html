Insulin glargine
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins) > [Long Acting Insulins] Date added to formulary : 30-12-2016
Formulary Status: AmberI  - AmberI 

Medicines Available:
Insulin glargine
Lantus®
NICE NG 17 - Lantus
[relaces TA 53]
Type 1 diabetes mellitus. Should only be considered for type 2 diabetes mellitus if:
the patient requires assistance from a carer or healthcare professional to administer their insulin injection
the patient's lifestyle is significantly restricted by recurrent symptomatic hypoglycaemic episodes or
they would otherwise need twice daily basal insulin injections in combination with oral antidiabetic drugs

AWMSG 0413 - Lantus
Treatment of type 1 diabetes mellitus in children aged 2 to less than 6 years.

Abasaglar®
AWMSG 3215 - Abasaglar
Treatment of diabetes mellitus in adults, adolescents, and children aged 2 years and above.
Abasaglar is a biosimilar and is approved within its licensed indication in accordance with NICE or AWMSG guidance for insulin glargine (Lantus), the reference product.
It is not recommended for use within NHS Wales outside of these circumstances.

Toujeo®
"Toujeo" - insulin glargine 300IU/mL in a pre-filled pen is recommended for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adults and children aged 6-18 years ONLY within the following patient groups;
  • Where the flexibility of injection timings (+/- 3 hours) due to proven poor glycaemic control attributable to problems with timing of daily insulin would be beneficial
  • Repeated and unpleasant hypoglycaemic episodes that significantly affect their way of life
  • Experiencing pain on injection, or having to split long-acting insulin doses for comfort
  • On split doses of long acting analogue insulin administered by a healthcare professional, where the second visit from the helathcare professional is for the sole purpose of delivering the second dose of insulin.
