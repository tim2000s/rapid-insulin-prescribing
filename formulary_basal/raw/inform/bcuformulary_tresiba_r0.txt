Insulin degludec
AWMSG 5196
Insulin degludec (Tresiba ® ) is recommended as an option for use within NHS Wales for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adults, adolescents and children from the age of 1 year.
This advice incorporates and replaces the AWMSG recommendation for insulin degludec (Tresiba®) for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adult patients where treatment with a basal insulin analogue is considered appropriate (advice number 2816, originally published October 2016).
