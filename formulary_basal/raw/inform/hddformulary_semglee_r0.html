Insulin glargine
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins) > [Insulin glargine] Date added to formulary : no date recorded
Formulary Status: S  - Specialist initiated 

Medicines Available:
Insulin glargine
August 2022 Current cost of insulin glargine pre-filled pens 5 x 3mL(Source Mims online ex VAT):
Semglee    £29.99
Lantus        £34.75
Abasaglar  £35.28
Insulin glargine should be prescribed by brandname to ensure that patients continue to receive the same device. 
Semglee® is now the most cost-effective insulin glargine for new start patients. 

Lantus® and Abasaglar® are reserved for patients already on insulin glargine. 
N.B. Toujeo (insulin glargine 300units/ml) is only available as an alternative for patients who are unable to tolerate  Semglee®, Abasaglar® or Lantus®.

AWMSG recommend prescribing by brand name to avoid confusion
 •Abasaglar 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
 •Abasaglar 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled disposable devices
 •Semglee 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
 •Semglee 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
 •Toujeo 300units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled DoubleStar pen
 •Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 10ml vials
 •Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
 •Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml OptiClik cartridges
 •Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled OptiSet pen
 •Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled SoloStar pen
 •Toujeo 300units/mL solution for injection 1.5mL pre-filled disposable devices
First line for new patients and selected existing patients where appropriate.
