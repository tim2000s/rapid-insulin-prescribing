Insulin glargine should be prescribed by brandname to ensure that patients continue to receive the same device.
Semglee ®  is now the most cost-effective insulin glargine for new start patients.
Lantus ® and Abasaglar ®  are reserved for patients already on insulin glargine.
N.B. Toujeo ( insulin glargine 300units /ml ) is only available as an alternative for patients who are unable to tolerate  Semglee ® , Abasaglar ® or Lantus ® .
