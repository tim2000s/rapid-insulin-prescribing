Insulin glargine
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins)
Date added to formulary : no date recorded
Formulary Status:
G     -  Green
Medicines Available :
Insulin glargine ( Abasaglar &  Lantus inc. SoloStar )
AWMSG Advice No 3215 recommends Abasaglar as an option for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adults, adolescents and children aged 2 years and above. Insulin glargine (Abasaglar®) should be prescribed within its licensed indication in accordance with NICE or AWMSG guidance for insulin glargine (Lantus®), the reference product.
Insulin glargine (Abasaglar®) should be prescribed by brand name to avoid automatic substitution and therefore help with pharmacovigilance.
• Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 10ml vials
• Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
• Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml OptiClik cartridges
• Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled OptiSet pen
• Lantus 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled SoloStar pen
• Abasaglar 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
• Abasaglar 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled disposable devices
