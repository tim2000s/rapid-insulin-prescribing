Insulin glargine
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins) > [Insulin glargine]
Date added to formulary : 24-12-2025
Formulary Status:
R    -  Specialist recommended
Medicines Available :
insulin glargine (Semglee - 1st choice) (Semglee - 1st choice)
• Semglee 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
Specialist Recommended for diabetes mellitus in adults, adolescents and children aged 2 years and above, as a more cost-effective alternative to Lantus and Abasaglar. It has a similar absorption profile to Lantus and Abasaglar. Dec 2025.
Prescribing Notes
Prescribe insulins and devices by brand name  in line with  NICE guidance  & ensure patients receive an administration device they have been trained to use. Manufacturers advise any switch between brands or formulation of insulin should be done under strict supervision as change in dose may be required.
Insulins are biologic medicines – biologic and biosimilar medicines should always be prescribed by brand name.  SPS - Medicines to prescribe by brand name in primary care- 2024
