Insulin degludec
06.01.01.02 (Intermediate- and long-acting insulins)
Date added to formulary : 10-02-2017
Formulary Status:
A     -  Amber
Medicines Available :
Insulin degludec ( Tresiba )
AWMSG Advice No 2816 recommends insulin degludec (Tresiba) as an option for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adult patients where treatment with a basal insulin analogue is considered appropriate.
Insulin degludec (Tresiba) is not recommended for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adolescents and children from the age of 1 year.
Note Tresiba is available in FlexTouch pens as both 100units/ml and 200units/ml - the strength must be specified when prescribing and in patient records.
AWMSG 5196 : Insulin degludec (Tresiba ® ) is recommended as an option for use within NHS Wales for the treatment of diabetes mellitus in adults, adolescents and children from the age of 1 year (added 7/11/22)
• Tresiba FlexTouch 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
• Tresiba FlexTouch 200units/ml solution for injection 3ml pre-filled pen
• Tresiba Penfill 100units/ml solution for injection 3ml cartridges
