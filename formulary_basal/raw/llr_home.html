MHRA Drug Safety Update: June 2022

Please see the June DSU which this month covers reduced vitamin B12 level associated with metformin, the Roche Accu-Chek Insight insulin pump and an update on COVID-19 vaccines and medicines. 

Freestyle Libre

There have been a number of changes to the prescribing and supply of Freestyle Libre in UHL and across LLR. Please see the entry on the formulary and the LLR APC website for further information. 

  • National Arrangements from NHS England
  • LLR Algorithm

Please note, these changes do not affect the eligible cohort of patients which are in the LLR position statement.

MHRA Drug Safety Update May 2022

Please see the May 2022 Drug Safety Update which includes information on Denosumab in patients aged under 18, and an update on COVID-19 vaccines and medicines.

MHRA Drug Safety Update March 2022

Please see the March 2022 Drug Safety Update which includes information on cladribine, amiodarone, metformin in pregnancy and an update on COVID-19 vaccines and medicines. 

COVID-19 vaccines and medicines: updates for February 2022

Please see the Feb 2022 update for info on approval of Novavax COVID-19 vaccine (Nuvaxovid), COVID-19 antivirals in pregnancy & Summaries of Yellow Card reporting and other recent MHRA publications

 

Shortage of Dulaglutide (Trulicity®)

Please see the LLR APC memo on the shortage of dulaglutide 3mg and 4mg pens. If required, advice can be sought from secondary care via Consultant Connect
