The APC Formulary meetings are held bimonthly.
For information on formulary additions or amendments please see below:

Request a new medicine or formulary amendment

Requests for new medicines to be added to the formulary may be made by Consultants, GPs and other appropriate senior clinicians within the Nottinghamshire health community. 

The application form is available to download from here:

If you require support or further details of the process please contact the team by emailing nnicb-nn.nottsapc@nhs.net. For simple requests and traffic light reclassifications, an abbreviated form is available from the team. Requests should be submitted at least three weeks before the meeting.
