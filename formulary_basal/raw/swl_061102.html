06.01.01.02    Intermediate- and long-acting insulins
Insulin degludec 100 units/ml (Tresiba®)
(3ml pen-fill cartridge)
Formulary

Insulins to be prescribed by brand name

WARNING: Two strengths of insulin degludec (Tresiba)

The dose titration and stabilisation will be undertaken by the specialist team with the first 4-6 weeks’ supply prescribed. Prescribing is subsequently continued by GPs/prescribers with advice available from community DSNs.

Review by specialist diabetes team to take place after 3 months. Local Risk assessment required to minimise prescribing & dispensing errors with the 2 strengths.


100units/ml is recommended for people with type 1 diabetes: to be used

  • If there is a nocturnal hypoglycaemia, DKA, or
  • poor 24-hour basal insulin cover with insulin glargine or
  • if flexible administration is required

 

 

 
 MHRA safety alert on availability of 2 strengths
 
Insulin degludec 200 units/ml (Tresiba®)
(3ml pre-filled pen)
Formulary

Insulins to be prescribed by brand name

WARNING: Two strengths of insulin degludec (Tresiba)

 

The dose titration and stabilisation will be undertaken by the specialist team with the first 4-6 weeks’ supply prescribed. Prescribing is subsequently continued by GPs/prescribers with advice available from community DSNs.

Review by specialist diabetes team to take place after 3 months.

Local Risk assessment required to minimise prescribing & dispensing errors with the 2 strengths.


200 units/ml is recommended for people with type 1 or type 2 diabetes: to be used

  • If patient is on doses of basal insulin > 80 units daily and it is being used in order to reduce the number of injections.

Insulin Glargine 300 units/ml (Toujeo doublestar®)
(3ml pre-filled pen)
Formulary

Insulins to be prescribed by brand name

Toujeo DoubleStar is reserved for patients on >80 units Toujeo 300units/ml insulin daily

The dose titration and stabilisation will be undertaken by the diabetes specialist team with the first 4-6 weeks supplied by them.


Restricted to patients with uncontrolled HbA1c who require basal insulin and have:

  • High insulin dose with significant insulin resistance > 80 units per day and require a simplified injecting regime.
  • Recurrent episodes of nocturnal hypoglycaemia (despite dose adjustment)

Not bioequivalent to insulin glargine 100 units/ml dose for dose. Switches to Toujeo® from other insulins require dose adjustment (see SPC and below for further information)
