Long-acting insulins

NHS Improvement Patient Safety Alert

 November 2016: Risk of severe harm and death due to withdrawing insulin from pen devices. A patient safety alert has been issued to warn NHS providers of the risk of severe harm and death if an insulin needle and syringe is used to administer insulin withdrawn directly from a pen device or replacement cartridge.

Drug Safety Update

September 2020: Insulins (all types): risk of cutaneous amyloidosis at injection site

April 2015: High strength, fixed combination and biosimilar insulin products: minimising the risk of medication error

A National Patient Safety Alert (NPSA) was issued on the 8th of December 2023 about the "Potential for inappropriate dosing of insulin when switching insulin degludec (Tresiba®) products". Please refer to the NPSA for further information on the identified safety issue, clinical information and actions required.

All strengths of Suliqua® (insulin glargine + lixisenatide) are being discontinued in March 2025 (100units/ml /33micrograms/ml and 100units/ml /50micrograms/ml solution for injection pre-filled SoloStar pens). Please see here on theSPS Medicines Supply Tool for more information and actions.

The Levemir® (insulin detemir) range will be discontinued at the end of 2026. Patients should be reviewed and changed by the end of September 2026. For further information on the medicines supply notification on SPS please click here. For the national clinical guidelines on managing switching please click here.

Insulin degludec

A National Patient Safety Alert (NPSA) was issued on the 8th of December 2023 about the "Potential for inappropriate dosing of insulin when switching insulin degludec (Tresiba®) products". Please refer to the NPSA for further information on the identified safety issue, clinical information and actions required.

Tresiba Flextouch® 100units/ml was discontinued in January 2026

Tresiba® should be reserved for patients who are difficult to control and need large insulin doses. Patients must meet the following criteria 

  • has tried other basal insulin regimens as recommended in NICE NG17 
  • risk of hypoglycaemia because of reduced awareness
  • more than 2 documented DKA related admissions in the last 12 months
  • initiated by a consultant only

Insulin glargine 300units/ml

Toujeo is a higher strength 300unit/ml formulation. It is used for patients who are difficult to control and where large insulin doses are needed. When switching from a 100unit/ml formulation, dose modification and close monitoring is needed in the transition period.

Toujeo Doublestar pre-filled pen deliver doses from 2-160 units in steps of 2 units. Care must be taken to ensure that the correct device is prescribed. Doublestar pre-filled pen is recommended for patients requiring 20 units or more daily.

For specialist initiation with specialist defined as secondary care prescribers, GPs, Specialist DSNs, and specialist pharmacists or nurses in primary care (e.g. practice or spoke nurses) who have completed PITstop or equivalent.
