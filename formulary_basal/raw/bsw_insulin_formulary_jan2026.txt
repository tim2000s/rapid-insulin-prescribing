                                                Type 1 Diabetes
                                                  Basal-Bolus
 NICE recommends a basal–bolus regimen for all adults with type 1 diabetes, with twice-daily insulin
 detemir as the preferred basal insulin. However, where once-daily dosing is clinically appropriate and
 supports adherence, insulin glargine may be considered as an alternative basal insulin, provided
 glycaemic targets are achieved and patient preference is taken into account. Do not offer adults newly
 diagnosed with type 1 non-basal-bolus insulin regimes. [NICE 2022]
